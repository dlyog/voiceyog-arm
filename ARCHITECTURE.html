The problem

Kokoro is the gold standard for small TTS. It also wants a GPU and 3.4 GB — and ships a dozen voices you will never use.

If you have banked your own voice — before illness takes it, or simply because it is yours — you need exactly one. Forever. On hardware you own, offline.

Distilled from

Kokoro-82M Apache-2.0

The gold standard for lightweight TTS. We did not replace it — we specialised it: one voice, re-tuned for Arm CPUs, so it runs faster in less memory on hardware people already own.
